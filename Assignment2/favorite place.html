Manali - My favorite place

Manali is one of my favorite places in India.
It is a beautiful hill station surrounded by mountains and nature.
Manali is a wonderful place for travelling, while its peaceful environment makes it special.

About the Place

Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh.
It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, rivers and pleasant weather.
It is a popular desination for nature lovers and adventure lovers.

Basic Information

    Place      : Manali
    Location   : Himachal Pradesh, India
    Best Time  : October to june
    Famous For : Mountains, Snow, Nature and Adventure
    Climate    : Cool and Pleasant
